About the Project

CareerPath AI is the AI-Powered Career Guidance, Job and Internship Matching System for University Students — a university Software Engineering project.

Project introduction

CareerPath AI is a web platform that helps university students and recent graduates discover suitable career paths, find relevant job and internship opportunities, analyze their skill gaps and receive personalized career development recommendations.

Background and purpose

University career services are often limited in capacity, and students rely on scattered online sources to plan their careers. The purpose of this project is to combine career assessment, opportunity matching and skill development into one intelligent, student-centered platform.

Problem statement

Many students graduate without a clear understanding of which careers match their abilities, which skills employers require, or where to find suitable entry-level opportunities. This leads to mismatched applications, longer job searches and underemployment among graduates.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To empower every university student with personalized, data-driven career guidance and fair access to relevant opportunities.

Our vision

A future where every graduate enters the workforce with clarity, confidence and the skills their chosen career demands.

Challenges students face when choosing careers

  • Limited awareness of career options related to their field of study.
  • Uncertainty about which skills employers actually require.
  • Difficulty finding internships suited to their year of study.
  • Lack of personalized mentorship and career counselling.
  • No structured way to plan learning or track applications.

How the platform solves these problems

Career assessment

Analyzes academic background, skills and interests to recommend suitable career paths.

Smart matching

Ranks jobs and internships by match percentage based on the student profile.

Skill gap analysis

Compares current skills with career requirements and highlights priorities.

Learning roadmap

Creates a step-by-step plan with courses, resources and milestones.

Application tracking

Keeps every application, status and interview in one organized dashboard.

Main project objectives

  1. Design a student profile system capturing academic and skill information.
  2. Develop an AI-based career recommendation module.
  3. Implement job and internship matching with match scoring.
  4. Provide skill gap analysis and personalized learning roadmaps.
  5. Enable students to track their job and internship applications.

Platform benefits

  • For students: clear direction, relevant opportunities and focused skill development.
  • For universities: better graduate employability and career service reach.
  • For employers: access to better-prepared and well-matched candidates.
