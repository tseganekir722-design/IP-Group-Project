Career guidance that understands students

Choosing a career is one of the biggest decisions a university student makes. Many students graduate without clear direction, unaware of which roles match their strengths or which skills employers expect. CareerPath AI turns your profile into clear, personalized guidance so you can plan with confidence from your first year to graduation.

How the system works

  1. 1. Create your profile

    Add your university, department, academic year, skills and career interests.

  2. 2. Take the career assessment

    Answer questions about your strengths, interests and preferred work environment.

  3. 3. Receive AI recommendations

    Get ranked career paths, matched jobs and internships with clear match scores.

  4. 4. Close your skill gaps

    Follow a personalized learning roadmap and track your applications in one place.
